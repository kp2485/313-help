# Free Narcan in the Macomb County places a DDOT or SMART bus reaches (Center Line, Clinton Township, Mount Clemens,
# New Baltimore, Roseville, St. Clair Shores, Sterling Heights, Warren). Batch "macomb-narcan", written 2026-10-01
# (Kyle: "do the same for the macomb county narcan locations", after Oakland County's map was ingested).
#
# Macomb County publishes no Narcan locations of its own: no layer on its GIS portal (its Behavioral Health Providers
# map has no Narcan sites) and no list on macombgov.org. The nearest equivalent is the list kept by the group that
# stocks the boxes: Families Against Narcotics (now "Face Addiction Now"), "Free Narcan RescueBox Locations",
# https://www.faceaddictionnow.org/rescuebox, a Looker Studio table of 119 boxes statewide that a script cannot read.
# It was read in a browser on 2026-10-01 (name, address, box type, where it sits, hours; it carries no phone, no
# coordinate and no person's name). Its 28 Macomb rows inside the 75 places are the FAN lines below; Lenox is outside.
# FAN lines therefore carry entry_method web (read by a person in a browser), not auto_check.
#
# Checked as Oakland's map was (DECISIONS 2026-10-01): every FAN address was matched by the US Census Bureau's
# geocoder; where it did not match, or looked wrong, the host's own website was read:
# - BioMed: FAN writes "31581 Gratiot Ave"; BioMed's own contact page (biomedmat.org/index.php/contact-us/) says 31851.
# - 41A District Court: FAN writes "Dodger Park Rd"; the City's page (sterlingheights.gov/421/41-A-District-Court) says Dodge Park Rd.
# - Macomb County Alano Center: FAN writes ZIP 48035; its own site (macombalanoclub.org, read in a browser) says 48036.
# - Sterling Heights Lions Club: FAN writes ZIP 48088 (Warren); the Census Bureau's match of the street says 48313.
# - McLaren Macomb: FAN writes "1000 Harrington St ... 48073"; McLaren's own page says 1000 Harrington Blvd, 48043.
#   The McLaren line below is McLaren's own (its news page says the FAN dispenser at the ER entrance is free to anyone).
# - Street forms completed from the Census Bureau's match of the same address: "21201 13 mile" -> 21201 E 13 Mile Rd,
#   "44575 Garfield" -> 44575 Garfield Rd, "14500 12 Mile Rd" -> 14500 E 12 Mile Rd, "31960 Little Mack" -> 31960
#   Little Mack Ave, "12828 canal road" -> 12828 Canal Rd.
# - Roseville Police Department: no Census match; the City's page (roseville-mi.gov/312/Locations) confirms 29753 Gratiot Ave.
# - Relax Inn, Macomb County Alano Center and McLaren have no Census point; Relax Inn has no website of its own.
# Hours are FAN's, written in the plain form the importer reads ("M-FRI-9AM-5PM" -> Mon-Fri 9am-5pm; no time changed). FAN gives "24HRS" for four college buildings, which may be locked at night, so those say call
# first; BioMed's Saturday hours in FAN's table are inverted ("SAT-6PM-9AM"), so only its weekday hours are kept.
# Box words: White Cabinet -> "a white cabinet", Small Wall Mount -> "a small box on the wall", Floor Dispenser ->
# "a stand-up box". FAN's table says "Free Narcan"; it says nothing about ID or questions, so neither do these lines.
#
# Not lines: CARE of Southeastern Michigan and the Clinton Counseling Center jail program give a free kit only after a
# class; ACCESS Point (Sterling Heights) offers naloxone on an order form that never says free; Macomb Community College
# pages only show classes. The Oakland map's Sacred Heart box in St. Clair Shores is already ingested.
#
# Other lines: Live Rite's machine (its home page: "Free NARCAN available at our office M-F 9-4 or in the Vending Machine
# anytime during meetings"); McLaren Macomb's dispenser (mclaren.org/main/news/mclaren-macomb-partners-with-fan-to-add-free-narca-5140);
