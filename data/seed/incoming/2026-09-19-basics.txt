# Notes for the steward:
# - Most Holy Trinity clothing room is a new service at the same place as sal_most_holy_food_pantry (1050 Porter). The page
#   says it is "generally" open Thursdays 11-1 when the pantry is open, so it is entered as written and says "call first".
# - The three Crossroads lines are new services at the same place as sal_crossroads_sunday_meal (2424 W Grand Blvd). The
#   resource list (/clients) names clothing, diapers, formula, baby clothing and the computer lab, and shows the phone and
#   street number. Hours come from the weekly page https://www.crossroadsofmichigan.org/thisweek (week of Sep 14-20, 2026),
#   which changes every week, so they are kept as text (call first) rather than a schedule. Neither page says the items are
#   free, so the lines do not say "free".
# - Human-I-T sells devices ("starting at $73"); classes and internet setup are free. Listed as low cost, not free.
# - Detroit Public Library Main: the page does not print a ZIP (Connect 313 gives 48202; a person should confirm before
#   filling it in). DPL pages disagree on the computer guest-pass fee ($1 vs no charge), so the line does not say computers
#   are free for everyone. The heat/cold respite wording is from https://detroitpubliclibrary.org/cooling-centers.
