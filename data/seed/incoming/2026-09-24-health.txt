# Health care in the 71 places added on 2026-09-24 (every city and township a DDOT or SMART bus reaches, outside
# Detroit, Hamtramck, Highland Park and Dearborn): clinics, dentists, eye care, care in pregnancy, HIV and STI tests,
# mental-health support places, and urgent care. Batch "health", written 2026-09-24.
# Method: every page was read on the organization's own site (or its own page on its county's official site) on
# 2026-09-24, fetched one page at a time with the pipeline's own honest user agent (pipeline/src/page-match.ts
# fetchPage), and each line's phone and street number were then checked on its source_url with the same strict
# matcher `pnpm check:sources` uses (listingOnPage). Web search, and HRSA's public list of health-center sites, were
# used ONLY to find organizations; no fact on any line comes from a search snippet, HRSA's list, or a directory.
# No emergency rooms (another step), no crisis lines, no Detroit Health Department rows.
#
# Notes for the steward:
# - Cost words: a line says "free" only where the owner's page says free or no cost. FQHC lines say "you pay based on
#   your income" only where the owner's page says sliding fee or discounts by income. Every health.urgent line says
#   it is NOT free: Corewell's and Henry Ford-GoHealth's pages say "insurance and self-pay".
# - Facts spread over several pages of one owner's site: the source_url is the page that shows both the phone and the
#   street address; the rest came from the same site:
#   * Western Wayne Family Health Centers: walk-ins, sliding fee and the dental emergency walk-in hours are on
#     wwfhc.org/services/ and wwfhc.org/cost-2/; addresses, phones and each suite's hours on wwfhc.org/locations/.
#     WWFHC's PrEP page (wwfhc.org/prep/) gives no address, so there is no health.sexual line for WWFHC.
#   * Oakland County Health Division: walk-in only, the fees ($10 visit, $12 per vaccine) and "no one will be denied"
#     are on .../public-health-services/clinic-services; what the sexual-health visit covers is on
#     .../clinic-services/sexual-health-services. The SOUTHFIELD phone (248-424-7000) is printed only on
#     .../about/contact-us, so both Southfield lines use that page as source_url.
#   * Macomb County Health Department, St. Clair Shores shots: hours are on .../family-health-services/immunization-clinic,
#     which prints no St. Clair Shores phone; that line uses the Health Department page (address + 586-466-6800).
#   * Honor Community Health: "we serve all patients regardless of ability to pay" and income-based discounts are on
#     honorcommunityhealth.org/honor-cares-program/; school centers "open to non-students" and ages 3-21 are on
#     honorcommunityhealth.org/schoolhealth/.
#   * Wellness Plan: OB/GYN days per site and "underinsured and uninsured" are on wellplan.com (home page);
#     addresses, phones and hours on wellplan.com/contact-locations/.
#   * Downriver Community Clinic: "primary care at no cost" and "proof of employment or school enrollment" are on
#     downrivercommunityclinic.org/about; address and phone on the home page.
#   * Crossroads Clubhouse: "participation is free ... no referral is needed" is on MCCMH's page
#     (mccmh.net/crossroads-clubhouse/), which gives the phone but not the address; the clubhouse's own site has both.
#   * CNS drop-in centers (F.A.I.R., Comfort Zone Unlimited) are listed on cnshealthcare.org/locations as
#     "Consumer-Run Drop-In Centers" with address, phone and hours; the page says nothing about who may come, so
#     both lines say to call first.
# - Rows with NO address, on purpose (a nurse comes to you, or the place is not named on the page): the three county
#   home-visiting lines (health.prenatal) and Oakland's children's vision screening line (its page says "at our
#   offices" without naming one). They carry a phone only. Healthy Families America Wayne's phone is a 313 number and
#   its page names no office; it serves all of Wayne County "including Detroit" - keep or drop as the steward prefers.
# - Two rows share an address with a different organization on purpose: Oakland County's clinic and Honor Community
#   Health both work at 1200 N Telegraph (Pontiac) and 27725 Greenfield (Southfield); Wayne County's public health
#   clinic and Wayne County Healthy Communities (an FQHC) both work at 33030 Van Born (Wayne). Different phones.
# - Macomb's family-planning lines (birth control, pregnancy tests) are health.clinic, a second service at the same
#   Health Department doors as the shots lines, with their own phone and hours.

#   30 days (read 2026-09-24); the three lines carry that as a notice. Re-check in late October.
