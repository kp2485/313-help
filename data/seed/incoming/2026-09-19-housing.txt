# Per-row notes for a steward:

The lawyers sit at 36th District Court, room 417 (421 Madison St), but that address is not on the City's page. 866-313-2520 is the same number as sal_housing_helpline (shelter.emergency); this row is the eviction-lawyer door to it. Funding is in question (April 2026 news); re-read the page each release.

The page names HHFS as the running department but the contact block is still HRD; we used the contact block. Existing org id for HRD is org_detroit_hrd; the importer will make a new org id, so merge by hand.
# - HOPE: dated deadline in notice (4:30pm Nov 6, 2026). The same City page still prints UCHC's old address and a 313-405-7726 number; we did not copy either.
# - DLBA Buy Back: the lobby hours are written as the page says; Buy Back office hours are by phone only, so the row stays "call first".
# - Detroit Housing Commission: the same page also lists the Administrative Office at 1301 E Jefferson; we list the Customer Service Center. Section 8 waiting list closed (notice).
# - Dearborn Home Rehabilitation Program: 313-943-2150 is the City's main line (not a program line); the page also says call 311. Applications are picked up in person.
# - detroitmi.gov answered our named script (200) today, but docs/02 says it often serves a bot challenge. If check:sources is blocked there, the Detroit rows need a person's browser check.
# - Lakeshore Legal Aid's Dearborn walk-in office and its CALL line are in 2026-09-19-legal-id.txt (category legal); they also serve housing cases.
# - UCHC (300 River Place, Suite 1200) is held, not listed: its contact page says Mon-Fri 8:30am-5:00pm and its home page says 8:30am-4:30pm. Its hotlines are offered as screen hotlines instead.
