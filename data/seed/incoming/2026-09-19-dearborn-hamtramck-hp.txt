# Free help in Dearborn, Hamtramck and Highland Park (service area widened 2026-09-19, see DECISIONS).
# Each was read on the organization's own site (or its own page on dearborn.gov) on 2026-09-19, with WebFetch.
# Web search was used only to find the sites; no fact comes from a search snippet or a directory.
# Notes:
# - Western Wayne Family Health Centers: the sliding-fee sentence is on their home page (wwfhc.org); the address,
#   phone and each suite's hours are on the locations page used as source_url. The two suites keep different hours.
# - Dearborn Community Food Pantry and Helping Hand: monthly days (3rd Saturday; 1st and 3rd Wednesday) do not parse
#   as weekly hours, so they arrive call-first; a MONTHLY schedule matching the page was then added by hand.
# - Henry Ford Centennial Library: the page says Sunday 1-5, closed Sundays May 25 - August 31. Sunday was added by hand
#   as a schedule valid 2026-09-01 to 2027-05-24, so the app shows it closed on summer Sundays unless someone renews it.
# - Narcan boxes: the City's overdose page gives the places but no hours; the phone is the Public Health office.
#   They were then set to availability "unknown" by hand, like DHD's stations (you can't call a box).
# - DRMM Oasis: the locations page gives the address and phone and calls it housing for returning citizens; the
#   housing-services page (drmm.org/services/housing-services/) calls it an emergency shelter for men, 90 beds.
