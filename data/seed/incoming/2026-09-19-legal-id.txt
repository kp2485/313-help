# Free and low-cost legal help (legal) and getting a City ID (ids) in Detroit, Dearborn, Hamtramck and Highland Park.
# Source notes: docs/research/2026-09-19-new-help/legal-and-id.md. Every page was read on 2026-09-19.
# Facts come only from each owner's own page. The phone digits and the street number are both on the source_url page; phone-only rows need only the phone.
# Birth certificates: the Detroit Health Department does NOT issue them (its Vital Records Division closed in December 2013). The Wayne County Clerk does, but its site refused our script, so that row is held for a person.
# Per-row notes for a steward:
# - Lakeshore Legal Aid, Dearborn: the only walk-in legal office found in our four cities. It also takes housing cases (eviction); consider showing it on the rent screen too.
# - Lakeshore Legal Aid, Canfield: the Find Legal Help page says "most offices are open for walk-ins", but this office's own note says no walk-ins; we followed the office note (appointment only).
# - Neighborhood Legal Services Michigan: on its contact page the tel: links are swapped (the visible 313-964-1975 links to 313-937-8291). We used the visible text. The Elder Law & Advocacy Center office is in Redford (outside our cities), so it is phone-only.
# - Michigan Immigrant Rights Center: the page gives three different hour statements; the schedule is the line hours as written, so the row stays "call first".
# - International Institute: NOT free. The first consultation costs $50 (said in "what"). 313-871-8600 ext. 234 is the legal appointments extension, not a person's line.
# - Freedom House Detroit: phone only, no address, on purpose. The owner publishes only a PO box; the street address on the federal (EOIR) roster must never be used (shelter rule). Services are "affordable", not stated as free.
# - Friend of the Court: source is the court's own PDF (3CC-FOC-4247PW, dated 7/1/2026) because 3rdcc.org pages are a script shell. If check:sources cannot read a PDF, a person must check it by eye.
# - Detroit ID: the fee is not on the program page (a 2024 City release says $10; older aggregator text says $25), so no price is shown. detroitmi.gov answered 200 today but may serve a bot challenge; if so, needs a person's browser check.
# - Detroit ID at Patton: a different organization's service in the same building as sal_detroit_parks_patton_community_center.
