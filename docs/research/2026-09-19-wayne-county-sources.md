# Wayne County and related public sources: research notes

2026-09-19. This answers Kyle's question: "Can we get data from Wayne County too?" It is research only. Nothing here has been built, and no record containing a person's name was downloaded. All script requests used a user-agent that named this project and said it is a zero-PII civic directory. Where a site refused that request, this doc says so. The built-in browser was used only to read public pages the way a visitor would.

## Summary and recommendation

**Yes, but most of the useful data is not published by Wayne County itself.** Two of the three gaps in docs/13 now have a real source. Foreclosures do not.

1. **Build first: crashes involving people walking or biking, from SEMCOG's `Crash Locations, 2015-2024` layer.** It has 1,335,379 crash points across the 7-county region, and every crash has `PEDESTRIAN`, `BICYCLE`, `KCOUNT`, `YEAR` and `community` fields. It has no names. SEMCOG's own server does the adding up: statistics grouped by `community` and `YEAR` worked, and statistics filtered to a polygon (a neighborhood outline) also worked. That means the same pattern as `ingest-neighborhoods.ts` would apply, and no crash record is downloaded. It covers all four cities, and 2024 is the newest year. **Before we use it, Kyle has to decide about the license.** SEMCOG's Copyright License Agreement grants a perpetual, royalty-free license to reproduce, modify and publish. But it also requires the notice "Copyright © [year] SEMCOG. All Rights Reserved. Reproduction or Use Without Permission is Prohibited," and it includes a one-way indemnification clause and a clause saying it grants no third-party rights (the underlying crash reports belong to the Michigan State Police). A short email to SEMCOG (infocenter@semcog.org) asking them to confirm that this use is fine would settle it.
2. **Second: help listings from Wayne County services for Dearborn, Hamtramck and Highland Park.** These are the Wayne County Health Department's clinic and WIC sites, Veterans Services, Senior Services (Dearborn only), Wayne Metro service centers, the Treasurer's foreclosure-prevention partners, and the right homelessness entry point for each city. These are Tier B, entered by a steward. **Two official sites refuse honest script requests** (waynemetro.org and outwaynehomeless.org both return HTTP 403), so `pnpm check:*`-style checkers can't read them, and a person has to check them by hand.
3. **Evictions: city-level only, for now.** There is no open source by neighborhood or tract for Detroit. The 36th District Court's case system forbids bulk downloads and holds tenant names, so we must not use it. The Eviction Lab does not track Detroit or anywhere in Michigan. The State Court Administrative Office publishes landlord-tenant filings per court per year, and each court is one city: 36th = Detroit, 19th = Dearborn, 31st = Hamtramck, 30th = Highland Park. That gives a citywide number only, and it would have to be copied by hand from a dashboard. Getting tract-level counts would need written permission from a research group (Tier C).
4. **Tax foreclosures: no current aggregate is published.** The Treasurer publishes lists one parcel at a time, as PDFs. Those lists include "interested parties," which means names, so we don't download them. The only counts by municipality are for **2019** (Detroit 3,448 of 3,960 countywide; Hamtramck 16; Highland Park 63; Dearborn 8). The honest route is a FOIA request to the Treasurer for **counts only**, by municipality and year (or by parcel number with no names, which we'd count per neighborhood on the City's parcel layer). That needs Kyle's go-ahead.
5. **Wayne County's own open data is thin but has one useful layer.** `Parcels_AssessmentData` has 812,827 parcels countywide, updated 2026. It holds `OwnerName`, so we would only ever ask its server for counts. For example, the share of parcels with a 100% homestead (principal residence) exemption is a possible "owner-occupied" measure for Hamtramck, Highland Park and Dearborn, where the City of Detroit's layers don't reach. No license is stated.

**Watch out:** `auditor-waynecountygis.opendata.arcgis.com` is **Wayne County, Ohio**, not Michigan. Its layers include "ODNR Dams" and "Ag Security Areas_Wayne County Ohio." The Michigan county's hub is `data-wayne.opendata.arcgis.com`.

## B. Listings (free help)

Jurisdiction facts that decide who serves whom:
- **Public health:** Detroit has its own health department. Wayne County's health department covers the rest of the county, which includes Dearborn, Hamtramck and Highland Park. MALPH lists Wayne County's population as "1,173,185 (2020-outside of Detroit)": https://www.malph.org/directory/wayne-county-department-health-veterans-community-wellness
- **Seniors:** Wayne County Senior Services serves "34 communities in western Wayne County and in the downriver areas." Its service-area flyer lists Dearborn, and does not list Detroit, Hamtramck or Highland Park. Those three are served by the **Detroit Area Agency on Aging**, which lists "Detroit, Hamtramck, Highland Park, Harper Woods, Grosse Pointe…". The county's Home Delivered Meals page gives DAAA's number, 313-446-4444.
- **Homelessness entry point:** Detroit, Hamtramck and Highland Park use **CAM Detroit**, (313) 305-0311, https://camdetroit.org/ (the number is shown as text and readable by script). Dearborn uses the **Out-Wayne County CoC**, (734) 284-6999. Note: docs/02 calls 734-284-6999 an old Wayne Metro flyer number. It is actually the Out-Wayne CoC's current housing-crisis line.

## Notes

- **How we'd use SEMCOG crashes without downloading records:** POST `…/crash2024_10year/FeatureServer/0/query` with `geometry` = one neighborhood polygon (`inSR=4326`), `outStatistics` = sum of `PEDESTRIAN`, `BICYCLE`, `KCOUNT`, and `groupByFieldsForStatistics=YEAR`. Tested 2026-09-19 with a test box around Hamtramck. It returned only yearly sums, for example 2024: 17 pedestrian, 7 bike. One call per neighborhood per build, 205 plus the other cities, on the monthly schedule. Label it "police-reported crashes." `PEDESTRIAN` counts **crashes** involving a person walking, not people hurt; `PEDINJURY` counts people hurt.
- **Data lag:** SEMCOG's newest full year is 2024. MTCF already has 2025. SEMCOG normally republishes each fall; this layer was last edited 2025-10-13. The panel must show "through 2024" (honesty rule 7).
- **Server-side counts from layers that hold names** (`Parcels_AssessmentData`) follow the same rule we already use for City sales: only `outStatistics` or `returnCountOnly`, never `outFields`. Whether that is acceptable for a county layer with `OwnerName` is a call for Kyle. It matches the docs/13 principle, but it's a new source.
- **Dearborn, Hamtramck and Highland Park have no neighborhood layer.** Options: treat each city as one unit (Hamtramck is 2.1 sq mi, Highland Park 3 sq mi), or use Dearborn's 14 `District_v2` polygons. Nothing was found that residents would recognize as neighborhood names.
- **Two things for Kyle to decide:** (1) Is SEMCOG's license acceptable, or should we email SEMCOG first? (2) Should we send the Treasurer a FOIA request for tax-foreclosure **counts** by municipality and year, 2019 to 2026, with no names? The Treasurer has its own FOIA form, linked from waynecountymi.gov under Records.
- Sites that refused our honest script requests (HTTP 403): evictionlab.org, poverty.umich.edu, waynemetro.org, outwaynehomeless.org. We did not retry them with a different user-agent.
