# Lane F: keeping people housed (research for `housing.rent` and related)

2026-09-19. Research only; nothing in the repo was changed. Builds on `docs/research/2026-09-19-wayne-county-sources.md` (Wayne County Treasurer partners, Wayne Metro, Out-Wayne CoC); those are not repeated here except where something new was found.

**How this was checked.** Every "verified" fact below was read on the owner's own page with `curl -A 'Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; 313help-research; open-source civic directory)'` on 2026-09-19. WebSearch was used only to find candidate pages; any fact that appears only in a search result or news story is labelled that way. No staff names, emails or direct lines are recorded. No court case records were opened.

**Status key.** **V** = phone and street address both printed as plain text on the one page named (our checker can find both). **P** = partial (on the owner's page, but phone and address are not on the same page, or something is missing or contradictory). **U** = unverified (only on an aggregator, news story, or a page that refused our script).

## Overview: what changed since the docs were written

1. **UCHC has moved.** Its own contact page now says **300 River Place Dr, Suite 1200, Detroit, MI 48207**. It is closed to walk-ins; you call for an appointment. The City's HOPE page and the 36th District Court's landlord-tenant FAQ still show the **old address, 2727 Second Ave, Suite 313**. We should use UCHC's own page.
2. **300 River Place is now Detroit's eviction-defense building.** UCHC is in Suite 1200, Michigan Legal Services in Suite 1201, and Lakeshore Legal Aid's Detroit Eviction Prevention office in Suite 1000. None of them take walk-ins.
3. **Right to Counsel.** UCHC's page says UCHC "administers the Right to Counsel program" with Michigan Legal Services, Lakeshore Legal Aid, and Legal Aid and Defender. The City's Office of Eviction Defense (in the Law Department) runs the program. The way in is the Detroit Housing Resource HelpLine, 866-313-2520, or showing up to the first hearing at the 36th District Court (room 417 for in-person hearings). Eligibility: household income up to 200% of the federal poverty line, and the case must be in the 36th District Court (Detroit only). **Hamtramck, Highland Park and Dearborn have no right-to-counsel program.** A Bridge Detroit story from April 2026 (news, discovery only) asks whether Detroit can keep paying for it.
4. **CERA is gone.** `detroitevictionhelp.com` and `.org` now redirect to the City's Right to Counsel page. There is no City rental-assistance application open right now. For back rent, the paths still open are State Emergency Relief (MDHHS, only with a court summons or judgment), Wayne Metro (its site blocks scripts), and ACCESS in Dearborn and Hamtramck, which helps people apply for SER.
5. **Dated deadlines.** HOPE (Detroit property-tax exemption): **4:30 p.m. November 6, 2026**. The City will also take *new* 2025 HOPE applications until that same date. Detroit Critical Home Repair and LeadSafe pre-applications: **8 a.m. September 9 to 5 p.m. September 22, 2026**, which closes 3 days from now. There was an in-person HOPE fair **today**, 2026-09-19, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., at Patton Recreation Center, 2301 Woodmere. It is over before anything ships.
6. **Closed or dead:** Renew Detroit ("no additional phases or application periods"). The City's Life & Legacy (free wills) page returns "Content Not Found." The ACCESS/Dearborn RMU rent grant is a 2021 COVID page. The 0% Home Repair Loan is described in the past tense by LISC, and detroithomeloans.org is © 2014. `MichiganHousingLocator.com` now redirects to AffordableHousing.com, a commercial site, and MSHDA's own "Michigan Housing Locator" link points there too.
7. **Waitlists:** Detroit Housing Commission's HCV (Section 8) list is **closed**. MSHDA: "No Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) waiting lists currently open." Wayne County project-based lists (Lee Plaza I and II) opened August 5, 2026. Those are applied for through a lead agency, not online.
8. **detroitmi.gov answered our named script today (HTTP 200) on about 20 pages.** This contradicts the 2026-09-19 note in docs/02 that it serves a bot challenge. It may be intermittent. One page (Life & Legacy) returned 403 with a "Content Not Found" body, which looks like an unpublished page, not bot protection. `pnpm check:sources` should still expect failures.
