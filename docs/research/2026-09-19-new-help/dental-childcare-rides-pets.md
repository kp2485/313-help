# Lane J: dentist and glasses, child care help, rides and bus fare, pet care

Researched 2026-09-19. Every fact below comes from the owner's own page unless it is marked otherwise. Requests used curl with `-A 'Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; 313help-research; open-source civic directory)'`, plus WebSearch to find candidates. Saved pages are in `scratchpad/laneJ/` (`*.html` / `*.txt`, the name is in the "curl" column).

**Status key.** **V** = verified on the owner's own page: the phone and street address both appear as plain text on the checker URL, and so do the cost and hours we quote. **P** = partial: something is missing, stale, or contradicts another source (see Issues). **U** = unverified: the owner's page is blocked or missing.

**Kind.** **Place** = a street location people go to. **Link-out** = a program, phone line, or website with no public walk-in location.

Staff names, emails and direct lines were left out on purpose. Several owner pages list them: the UDM emergency page, the MDHHS health-plan ride PDF, the Vision To Learn Michigan page, and the MACS clinic page.

**A general finding for the checker:** the brief says detroitmi.gov blocks scripts. Today it did not: curl with our honest user agent got **200** on every detroitmi.gov page tried (DDOT fares, DDOT paratransit, DHD vision and hearing, DHD medical transportation, Detroit Animal Care, dog licensing, the Ride to Rise news post). Sites that **did** refuse scripts today are waynemetro.org and nspheadstart.org, which both returned 403 "Access to this page is forbidden".

## J1. Dentist and glasses (low-cost or free)

### Overview
- **Dental.** Clinics already in `resources.csv` carry most of the low-cost dental care. The table below says which of them do dental and adds the dental-only phone and hours where the owner prints them. The biggest new place is the **University of Detroit Mercy dental school** in Corktown. It offers a student clinic at 30–50% below private prices, takes Medicaid, has a walk-in emergency clinic that opens at 7:30 a.m., and has a children's clinic that needs no referral.
- **No dental in Dearborn, Hamtramck-only, or Highland Park from the FQHCs checked, with one exception.** ACCESS (Dearborn) lists no dental. Western Wayne's Dearborn site is medical only; its dentists are in Inkster, Taylor and Lincoln Park, all outside our area. The exception is Hamtramck: the Wayne County Healthy Communities Hamtramck Health Center is already listed and already says "doctor or a dentist".
- **Vision.** Free options are few and mostly for children. They are:
  - the Detroit Health Department's free vision screening, ages 3–18, by appointment;
  - Vision To Learn, which gives free exams and glasses **in schools and Head Start**. Families cannot call it for a visit, but it holds occasional library events;
  - Eye Care for Detroit (now "Eye Care for Michigan"), which gives free prescription glasses to low-income homebound seniors and "at-risk Detroiters", and runs a walk-in vision clinic opened March 2025.
  - Wayne Health / Kresge Eye Institute is specialty eye care, not a low-cost program, so it is excluded.
  - Lions Club programs could not be verified; see Gaps.
- **Free dental days:** none found on a stated recurring schedule. Covenant's mobile dental is free to patients without coverage but visits partner sites only, and they say it "is not available for individual patient request".

## J2. Help paying for child care

### Overview
- **The Child Development and Care (CDC) scholarship has moved to MiLEAP**, the state's new early-learning department. The official page is on michigan.gov/mileap, not MDHHS. You apply on MI Bridges or with a paper form at your local MDHHS office. The income chart is dated **August 2026**. At application, a family's gross monthly income must be at or below the "Entry Level" column, for example **$5,500 a month for a family of 4**.
- **Free PreK:** all 4-year-olds in Wayne County qualify for GSRP, per findprek.org, which Wayne RESA runs. The single enrollment route for Detroit, Dearborn, Hamtramck and Highland Park is **findprek.org**, with call or text help at **313-410-4588**.
- **Head Start in Detroit today** is split among several grantees, each serving certain ZIP codes. The Michigan Head Start Association directory (used only to find candidates) lists **Matrix, United Children & Family, New St. Paul Tabernacle, The Order of the Fishermen Ministry, Starfish, MiSide (formerly Development Centers + Southwest Solutions), Renaissance**, and **Wayne Metro** for Hamtramck, Highland Park and Dearborn.
  - Two sites refuse scripts (403): Wayne Metro and New St. Paul.
  - Renaissance's domain looks parked.
  - **Focus: HOPE no longer runs Head Start.** Its early learning program is now GSRP, "Strong Beginnings" for 3-year-olds, and tuition infant care.

## J3. Rides to medical appointments and cheaper bus fare

### Overview
- **Medicaid rides.** Medicaid health plans must give free rides to covered care. The current MDHHS contact sheet, **"Revised 05/14/2026"**, lists each plan's ride line and how many days ahead to book (2–3 days is typical). People on Medicaid **without** a health plan (fee-for-service) call **Modivcare's MI Medicaid line, 1-866-569-1902**. Modivcare's own page prints that number; no MDHHS page printing it was found today. MDHHS tells everyone with questions to call the **Beneficiary Help Line, 1-800-642-3195**.
- **DDOT.**
  - Reduced fare is **$0.50** for people 65+, people with disabilities, Medicare cardholders, and students. **No low-income fare program exists** on DDOT's pages.
  - **Ride to Rise:** K-12 students at Detroit schools ride free with a school ID. The City posted this Aug 13, 2026 as "a six-month initiative", so an end date is needed.
  - **"MetroLift" is now "DDOT Paratransit"**: $2.50 cash, book two weeks to one day ahead, runs 24/7. **DDOT Now** is the same-day version: 6am–7pm Mon–Sat.
- **SMART.** Reduced fare is $0.50 for 65+ and people with disabilities. **All K-12, college and university students ride fixed routes free** since May 4, 2026.
- **Senior rides.**
  - Dearborn Senior Transportation: 60+, $2 round trip.
  - The Senior Alliance: urgent-only rides for 60+ in Dearborn and western Wayne County.
  - DAAA's Information & Assistance line helps Detroit, Hamtramck and Highland Park seniors find rides.
  - MyRide2: a free call line that finds rides.
- **The DHD "Rides to Care" program (free rides for pregnant women and new moms) appears to have ended.** Its URL now redirects to a generic page that doesn't mention it. A news piece from Dec 2025 still gave 313-876-0000; that number is not on any City page now.

## J4. Low-cost vet care and pet food

### Overview
- **Detroit has three real low-cost vet options on owners' pages:**
  - Michigan Humane's Grosfeld Veterinary Clinic: urgent care, spay/neuter, wellness.
  - All About Animals' Detroit Wellness Center: walk-in vaccines at $20 each, with a van to its Warren spay/neuter clinic.
  - Michigan Anti-Cruelty Society's clinic on Joseph Campau at the Hamtramck line: low spay/neuter prices for Detroit, Highland Park and Hamtramck residents.
- **Free spay/neuter for Detroit dogs:** Project GRACE (Dog Aide, Bark Nation, Friends of DACC), with no income or breed limits.
- **Pet food:**
  - Michigan Humane's Caplan Family Pet Food Pantry: free, once every 30 days. The distribution calendar is not in the page text.
  - Dog Aide: delivers dog food to Detroit homes.
  - Friends for Animals of Metro Detroit (Dearborn's shelter): "Operation Feed Fido", by appointment.
- **Detroit Animal Care and Control** is mainly strays, adoptions and licensing. Its page says "Onsite veterinarians provide vaccinations, spay/neuter…" but not that the public can book them. It does offer a vet for rabies shots Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 11–2, as part of licensing.
- **Detroit Pit Crew** only rescues injured strays. **Detroit Dog Rescue** is an adoption shelter with no public vet or food service on its page. Neither fits this topic.
