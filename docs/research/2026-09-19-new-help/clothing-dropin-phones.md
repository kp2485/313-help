# Lane I: clothing and baby needs, daytime drop-in, phones and internet

Research only, done 2026-09-19. Nothing in the repo was changed. Raw pages are saved in `scratchpad/laneI/p/` (`<name>.html` plus a `.txt` extract for each).

**How this was checked.** Every fact below comes from the owner's own page, fetched with `curl -A 'Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; 313help-research; open-source civic directory)'`. Aggregators (findhelp, 211, Yelp, news, Idealist, the Wayne County prosecutor's resource guide) were used only to find candidates. When a fact appears only on one of those, the entry says so. When a site refused scripts, it was recorded and left alone, with no workaround. No staff names, emails, or direct lines are recorded, even where a page shows them (for example the Human-I-T class schedule and the Cass and NOAH pages).

**Status key**
- **verified**: the owner's page shows the service, and the phone and street address both appear as plain text on one page (the URL given). Hours are quoted as printed.
- **partial**: the owner's page confirms the service, but something is missing (no hours, no phone and address on the same page, or the page is older than 2025).
- **unverified**: the fact came only from an aggregator or news, or the owner's page couldn't be read by script.
- **browser-only**: the site blocks scripts (403, TLS refusal) or renders only with JavaScript. A person must check it.

"Existing" means an organization or row already in `data/seed/resources.csv` (or in `data/seed/incoming/*`). For those, this file suggests what to add rather than listing them again.

## I1. Free clothing and baby needs

**Overview.** Crossroads of Michigan and the Most Holy Trinity clothing room are the only new clothing sources with both current hours and a phone and address on one page. The Metropolitan Detroit Diaper Bank is the main source of free diapers in Wayne County, but it is a monthly online request with a drive-thru pickup that isn't published, so it can only be a link-out. It also covers period supplies and adult incontinence supplies. Goodfellows is the one recurring seasonal clothing program with a stated schedule (it covers Detroit, Highland Park and Hamtramck, not Dearborn). No owner page was found for regular free clothing, diapers or period products in **Dearborn** or **Hamtramck**, and none for school uniforms or supplies on a regular schedule. Many clothing closets are already in the seed data as part of pantries (see "Existing overlaps").

## I3. Free phones, low-cost internet, computers and digital help

**Overview.** For free phones, the safe link-out is the **Michigan Public Service Commission's** Lifeline page and its **official provider list**, which names the carriers and their free monthly plans. That lets us avoid linking to any carrier or reseller. USAC's page gives the eligibility rules. The consumer apply site (lifelinesupport.org) and FCC.gov both refuse scripted requests. The federal **Affordable Connectivity Program (ACP) has ended** (money ran out in 2024), so we should never show it. Among home-internet plans, **Access from AT&T** is readable and current. Comcast's page blocks scripts, Spectrum's service in our cities is unconfirmed, and WOW! has no Michigan low-income plan on its own site. Community Wi-Fi from the **Equitable Internet Initiative** covers the North End, **Highland Park and Hamtramck** (NEWCC) and Southwest Detroit (Grace in Action, $10 a month). For devices, **Human-I-T on W. 7 Mile** is a verified walk-in store and lab, and **DPL lends laptops and hotspots for free** to Detroit and Highland Park residents. For free computer use, DPL and Dearborn libraries, the **Crossroads lab**, and the Connect 313 hub list are the main options.
