# Lane G: free legal help and getting ID documents (research, 2026-09-19)

Scope: Detroit, Hamtramck, Highland Park, Dearborn. All pages were fetched on 2026-09-19 with `curl -A 'Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; 313help-research; open-source civic directory)'`. Raw copies are in `scratchpad/laneG/` (`*.html`, `*.txt`). No staff names, emails or direct lines are recorded. Only main and intake lines are listed. Phone numbers and hours are copied exactly as each page prints them.

**Status key**
- **V**: verified on the owner's own page. The phone and the street address both appear as plain text on the "checker URL."
- **P**: partial. The owner's page has some facts, but it lacks the phone or the address, or its facts are contradictory.
- **U**: unverified. The fact comes only from an aggregator or search result, or the page blocks scripts.

**Overview.** The strongest listings are:
- Lakeshore Legal Aid: a free civil legal hotline, plus Dearborn and Detroit offices.
- Legal Aid and Defender Association (LAD): its civil side is still active in 2026.
- Neighborhood Legal Services Michigan (NLSM).
- Four Michigan Legal Help Self-Help Centers in Detroit.
- Four DOJ-recognized immigration providers inside our cities: MIRC, IIMD, ACCESS and Freedom House. The EOIR roster was updated 09/13/26.
- The Wayne County Friend of the Court (FOC) walk-in lobby.
- Detroit ID, which is issued again at two sites.

Big corrections to the brief:
- **The Detroit Health Department does not issue birth certificates.** Its Vital Records Division closed permanently in December 2013. Detroit births are handled by the Wayne County Clerk.
- **The "Legal Hotline for Michigan Seniors" (Elder Law of Michigan) appears to be gone.** It is not on ELM's services page, and an aggregator says it was discontinued in June 2019. Seniors are now served by Lakeshore's CALL line and by NLSM's Elder Law & Advocacy Center.
- **There is no Secretary of State office in Highland Park.** "Highland" on the SOS list is Highland Township in Oakland County.
