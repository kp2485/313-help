## 5. A limit worth writing down

Neither layer publishes a `GlobalID`. The keys are the precinct's ordinal (`3rd`) and the fire company's short
label (`E-1`), which are the best permanent references each list has. If the City renumbers a precinct or
relabels a company, that reads to us as a new record: the old id is retired to
`data/ingested/<source>.retired-ids.csv` and never reused, and the new record gets a new id. That is the same
limit the Recreation Centers layer and Wayne County's My Map already have.
